-----------------------------Notes on CSS--------------------------------

CSS can be seen as a process of three steps: 

Capture
CO2 is separated from other industrial gases at the source, which can
happen in different ways: 

1. Post-combustion: capturing CO2 after the fuel has been burned.
2. Pre-combustion: Capturing CO2 before the fuel is burned. 
3. Oxygen Combustion: burning fuel in pure oxygen instead of air. 


Transport
The captured CO2 is compressed and transported to a storage site, 
often via pipelines or ships

Storage: 
The CO2 is injected into deep underground geological formations for
permanent storage, e.g. depleted oil and gas reservoirs.
